6th Slide - Key Executive Business Insights

1. Revenue Growth: The platform generated ~29.9M total revenue, with strong growth during 2017, indicating rapid marketplace expansion.
2. Seasonal Sales Spike: Revenue peaked in November 2017 (2.8M), likely driven by holiday season / Black Friday demand.
3. Category Dominance: The toys category dominates the marketplace, generating significantly higher revenue and order volume than any other category.
4. High-Volume Sales Model: Despite not being among the highest priced categories, toys generate revenue through extremely high purchase volume.
5. Strong Delivery Performance: Approximately 97.9% of orders were successfully delivered, indicating strong operational performance.
6. Logistics Reliability: About 7.7% of orders were delivered later than estimated, suggesting generally reliable logistics but with room for improvement.
7. Shipping Cost Structure: Shipping costs average 44.28 per item, representing ~82.9% of product price, suggesting many low-priced products with relatively fixed shipping costs.

7th Slide - Business Recommendations

1. Double down on the Toys Category

Since toys drive the majority of revenue and orders, the company should:
- expand toy inventory
- strengthen supplier partnerships
- prioritize marketing in this category

2. Improve Logistics Efficiency

Reducing the 7.7% late delivery rate can improve customer satisfaction.

Possible actions:
- optimize courier partnerships
- improve warehouse processing times

3. Reduce Shipping Cost Burden

Since shipping cost is very high relative to product price, Possible strategies:
- bundled shipping
- minimum order value for free shipping
- multi-item delivery optimization

4. Promote High-Value Categories

Categories such as la_cuisine and garden_tools have high average prices.
Marketing these products could increase average order value.
